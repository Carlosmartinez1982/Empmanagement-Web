Privacy notice

EMP Management ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This notice explains how we handle the personal information you give us through this website.

What we collect

When you use our quote form we collect your name, company, email address, phone number, type of premises, cleaning frequency and any message you include.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry, arrange a site survey and provide a quote. We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.

Where it is processed

This website is hosted by Vercel. Quote form submissions are passed to us by email through the form service FormSubmit. We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to deal with your request and any resulting contract.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email Carlos@empmanagement.co.uk and we will respond within one month.
